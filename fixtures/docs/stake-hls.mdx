# Stake HLS

Deposit native HLS into the staking vault. You receive hHLS 1:1 at the current exchange rate, which increases as rewards accrue.

## Deposit

1. Connect a wallet.
2. Approve the vault if you are depositing a wrapped asset.
3. Call `stake(amount)`.
4. Wait for the deposit receipt before treating hHLS as spendable.

## Rewards

Rewards are socialized into the hHLS rate. Helios does not pay a advertised APY on this page; read the vault share math on-chain.

## Leaving the vault

Unstake is available from the same UI. Exit timing, queues, and slashing are documented only at a high level here — see engineering before publishing user-facing wait times.
